## Question

Why is `loss_total ≈ 6.5` flat for 1000 steps despite healthy weights, normal
LR, no NaN/Inf?

## Hypotheses

1. LR too high → bouncing
2. GAN warmup not active → no D signal
3. Loss-term imbalance → one term dominating, pixel head starved

## Method

Four parallel CPU-only audits, no contact with running trainer:

- gradient contribution per term (last 50 steps avg)
- GAN warmup gate + LR schedule shape from ckpt metadata
- weight stats (mean/std/absmax/NaN/Inf/L2) per ckpt at {1k, 2.5k, 4k, 5.5k, 7.5k}
- full 7900-row loss curve binned 20 windows

### LR + GAN (ruled out)

- `--warmup-steps=20000`. Step 7800 → 12.2k to GAN. `loss_gan_*=0` is the gate, expected.
- `lr_g = 1.891e-4 = 0.945 × initial`. Cosine `T_0=50000, num_restarts=5`. 15% into first cycle. Schedule fine.
- Discriminator loaded, `requires_grad=False` until warmup. Spectral-norm weights at init. Will activate at step 20k.

### Weights (ruled out)

- Zero NaN/Inf across all 5 ckpts.
- No exploding (max absmax 13.07, single discriminator spectral-norm scalar).
- No dead (no std<1e-6 weight tensor).
- Block growth 9-11% over 6500 steps. Normal Adam drift.

### Visual cross-check

mean(|v6.1−GT|) per pixel:
- step 100: 10.82/255
- step 1000: 8.31 (best)
- step 7500: 9.10

Error map column intensity went 27.0 (step 1000) → 30.0 (step 7500). Drift confirmed visually.

## Decision

H3 confirmed. λ retune (commit `96e5349`):

| const | before | after | why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `_W_CHARBONNIER` | 1.0 | **5.0** | lift pixel ~5× |
| `_W_LPIPS` | 1.0 | **2.0** | keep prominent, balanced |
| `_W_VGG` | 1.0 | **0.15** | cut dominant ~6.7× |
| `_W_WAVELET` | 0.5 | **1.5** | freq-domain pixel signal |
| `_W_GAN` | 0.05 | unchanged | still gated |
| `_W_SOBEL` | 0.2 | **1.0** | edge term was silent |
| `_W_TEMPORAL` | 0.5 | **2.0** | tc was 0.5% |

Target: each major term 10-25% of gradient.

## Learnings

1. **Always audit gradient % per term before declaring a plateau.** Total or
   individual values lie. A 2% term will never drive learning regardless of
   how nicely its absolute number drops.
2. **MSVGG with per-layer weights summing to 3.2 silently inflates λ_msvgg by
   3.2×.** Anyone tuning it sees `λ=1.0` and assumes balanced. They're not.
   Bake the multiplier into the λ value or document it next to the constant.
3. **A single flat `loss_total` curve hides divergent per-component dynamics.**
   Need a gradient-contribution panel on the dashboard alongside the loss
   plot.
4. **Resume-from-best is the wrong instinct here.** The 7000 steps of
   perceptual progress aren't wasted — pixel head was starved. Apply the
   fix, continue from the current step, keep the perceptual gains.
5. **Stale `.pyc` survives Python restart on Windows.** Delete
   `__pycache__/*.pyc` before relaunching after editing a hot module.
6. **Dashboard needs an `events` annotation layer.** Mid-training
   interventions (λ retune, LR drop, schedule swap) need a dot on every
   chart with hover info. Without it, a future operator interpreting these
   curves has no idea something changed at step 8000. Tracked.
