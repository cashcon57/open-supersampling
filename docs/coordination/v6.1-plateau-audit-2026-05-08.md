# v6.1-pico-001 plateau audit (2026-05-08)

**TL;DR:** Training-crop loss has been flat at ~6.5 for 1000+ steps. Visual
inspection of the inflight viz strip showed the model peaked around step 1000
on the GT-distance metric and is mildly drifting away on the held-out viz
scene. Four parallel agent audits triangulated on a single root cause:
**`msvgg` was hogging 87% of the gradient signal**; charbonnier got only 1.4%.
Pixel-fidelity terms (charb, tc, wavelet) all flatlined at step ~400 because
their gradient contributions were <2%. Lambda retune applied at step ~7800.

## Cross-confirmed diagnosis

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Loss-term gradient audit | `msvgg` contributes 86.7% of `loss_total`, `charbonnier` 1.4%. msvgg's effective weight ~60× larger than charb's because the multi-scale VGG term carries internal per-layer weights summing to 3.2. |
| Full-history loss curve | charbonnier flatlined at step ~400 (~0.024), tc at ~400 (~0.017), wavelet at ~400 (~0.146), sobel at ~2400 (~0.096), msvgg at ~4400 (~1.49). lpips never plateaued — the only term still moving. |
| Visual inflight-viz inspection | mean(\|v6.1 − GT\|) was 8.31/255 at step 1000 (best), drifting to 9.10 at step 7500. Error map column intensity 27.0 (step 1000) → 30.0 (step 7500). |
| GAN + LR audit | LR cosine schedule is correct (0.945× initial, 15% into first cycle). GAN warmup at step 20K (currently 7.8K). Discriminator loaded but frozen. **Not the cause.** |
| Weight-statistics audit | Zero NaN/Inf across all checkpoints. Block growth 9-11% over 6500 steps — normal Adam drift. No exploding/dead layers. **Not the cause.** |

## Retune applied (`oss/sr/v6/losses.py:280-296`)

| Term | Before | After | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `_W_CHARBONNIER` | 1.0 | **5.0** | Lift pixel signal ~5× — was contributing 1.4% of gradient. |
| `_W_LPIPS`       | 1.0 | **2.0** | Keep perceptual prominent but balanced. |
| `_W_VGG`         | 1.0 | **0.15** | Cut dominant term ~6.7×. Internal per-layer weights still scale ×3.2, so effective contribution becomes ~5%. |
| `_W_WAVELET`     | 0.5 | **1.5** | Amplify frequency-domain pixel signal. |
| `_W_GAN`         | 0.05 | 0.05 | Unchanged (still gated by `warmup_step=20K`). |
| `_W_SOBEL`       | 0.2 | **1.0** | Edge sharpness was nearly silent. |
| `_W_TEMPORAL`    | 0.5 | **2.0** | Temporal-consistency contribution was 0.5%. |

Target: each major term contributes 10-25% of `loss_total` for balanced gradient flow.

## NOT changed

- LR schedule — cosine restart with `T_0=50000` is intentional for the 300K-step recipe.
- GAN warmup step (20K) — schedule is correct, will activate at step 20K.
- Resume-from-best (ckpt-1000) — would discard the perceptual progress lpips made; better to apply the retune from current step and continue.

## Followups

1. **Restart trainer** at the current best ckpt (auto-resume from latest). The trainer reads `losses.py` at startup; new lambdas pick up immediately.
2. **Held-out trajectory eval** is in-flight (codex dispatch `v61-held-out-trajectory`). Will populate the `score_log` for ckpts 1000/2500/4000/5500/7000/7500 so we have hard test-set numbers to compare against post-retune.
3. **Watch the next 500-1000 steps** post-retune for charbonnier movement. Expect it to drop from ~0.022 toward ~0.015-0.018 within 1000 steps if the diagnosis is correct. If it doesn't move, the model is genuinely capacity-bottlenecked at this resolution and the retune isn't enough — escalate to v6.1-pico-002 with more channels or v6.1-standard.
