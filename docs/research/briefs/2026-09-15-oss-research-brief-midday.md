### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal

- **Deformable 2D Gaussian Splatting for Efficient 4K Video Compression** (arXiv:2609.14129, submitted 2026-09-12, announced Tue 15) — Chenhao Zhang, Fengqing Zhu (Purdue). Real-time UHD video codec: coarse-to-fine **multi-scale 2DGS structure + lightweight deformation network** per GOP; beats H.265 on LPIPS with real-time decode. **Why it matters to OSS:** the closest external analogue to the v7 canvas found to date — a persistent 2D Gaussian field with a *deformation network* carrying content across frames in time, exactly the canvas-warp/v7 time-axis family. Their multi-scale (coarse-to-fine) 2DGS hierarchy is a direct candidate for #12 packed-canvas capacity allocation, and "real-time decode of a 2D-GS video representation" is external validation that 2D-GS temporal representations can hit real-time budgets. **Mirrored** (`2609.14129-deformable-2dgs-4k-video.pdf`, 1.5 MB, sha256 a5dd950c…). Screened in, watch-tier donor; deep-read candidate.
- **ConeGaussian: Anti-Aliased Gaussian Ray-Tracing for Generic Central Cameras** (arXiv:2609.13397, submitted 2026-09-11) — Deheng Zhang, Letian Shi, Runyi Yang, Zhendong Li, Lei Sun, Kanzhi Wu, Ajad Chhatkuli, Danda Pani Paudel, Luc Van Gool. Camera-model-agnostic anti-aliasing for Gaussian ray rendering: anisotropic pixel footprint derived from neighboring rays via the camera's native inverse mapping, closed-form cone-Gaussian convolution. **Why it matters to OSS (watch-tier):** independent anti-aliasing-at-rasterizer work in the same "filter the Gaussian footprint against the pixel's ray bundle" family as the OSS EWA/Mip-Splatting covariance story; their closed-form cone-convolution is a candidate cross-check for the OSS rasterizer's AA path if/when OSS supports non-pinhole cameras. Rejected as direct donor (ray-tracing NVS lane, not screen-space canvas).
- **Transforming harmonic coefficients for 3D splat compression** (arXiv:2609.15735, submitted 2026-09-14, cs.GR) — Tam Thuc Do, Philip A. Chou, Gene Cheung. Color-attribute compression: images from splats are linear in per-channel/per-SH/per-splat coefficients; orthonormalizing coefficients under an image-space inner product before coding yields **>2 dB gain**. **Why it matters to OSS (note-tier, #12-adjacent):** a principled rate-distortion-optimal basis for *canvas feature compression* — if OSS canvas channels (F-channel decoder features, per-D10 quantized state) ever need a streaming/LoD codec, this linear-basis result is the right first reference.
- **Screened and rejected (Tue-15 delta, GS-adjacent):** GP Implicit Surfaces as Participating Media (2609.14695 — offline radiative transfer theory), GaussAnything (2609.13859 — VR LoD reallocation; intent-conditioned resident-budget reallocation is *thematically* adjacent to #12/H004 but is a host-streamed OpenXR system, not a real-time canvas primitive), SkyAnchor (2609.13903 — aerial scene update), Scene Extrapolation via 3DGS (2609.13262 — 3D scene completion, not frames), Splat compression via harmonic coefficients (covered above), Hologram/Quadratic Phase GS + Gaussian Light Transport + mmWave Radar Splatting (all previously rejected, unchanged).

### Super-Resolution / Upscaling

- **GameSR: Real-Time Super-Resolution for Interactive Gaming** — Haseeb Ur Rehman, Shervin Shirmohammadi, Ihab Amer, Mohamed Hefeeda (SFU). *IEEE Transactions on Games*, **accepted July 2026** (author PDF surfaced this run; not on arXiv). Engine-independent SR operating **directly on encoded game frames** (cloud-gaming lane): reparameterized conv blocks (1×1→3×3→1×1 merged at inference), PixelUnshuffle down-and-up, lightweight ConvLSTM for temporal state, Charbonnier + perceptual losses, Torch-TensorRT FP16 with kernel fusion. **138K parameters, ~4 ms/frame at 2×→1080p (up to 240 FPS), 7–15 ms at 4K, 35–56% cloud bandwidth reduction; competitive no-reference perceptual quality vs DLSS/FSR without engine data.** **Why it matters to OSS:** (1) the *strongest published existence proof yet* that a ~10× smaller-than-OSS-student CNN can be competitive on game content — direct ammunition for the ≤1M student tier (#4) and the RepVGG/reparam student-backbone ablation (#9); (2) their ablation **removing ConvLSTM drops PSNR 5 dB on CS2** — quantified evidence that cheap recurrent temporal context is the single highest-leverage component in a tiny game-SR model, a concrete design datapoint for the student recipe; (3) engine-agnostic (no MVs, no G-buffers) — the anti-OSS bet on *where* information comes from, useful as a contrast row in positioning. **Mirrored** (`tg26-GameSR.pdf`, 5.1 MB, sha256 2bbfe650…). Deep-read recommended for the student-tier design review.

### Frame Extrapolation / Generation

- **Tile-based Frame Extrapolation (TFE)** — Liang Pu, Haodong Tian, Jiawei Zhang, Weitao Zhang, Yanwen Guo, Junqiu Zhu, Jie Guo (Nanjing U + Shandong U). **SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Conference Papers**, DOI 10.1145/3829340.3842212. Code + project site public (NJUCG/tile-based-frame-extrapolation, 1★, pushed Aug 24; site pushed Sep 15). Pipeline: two-path **adaptive warp** guided by target-time G-buffers → geometric mask identifies unreliable-reprojection regions → **sparse tiles batched through a lightweight Tile Restoration Network (TRNet)** → residual applied inside the mask → **shadow-buffer-guided reshade pass** for moving shadows. Numbers (RTX 5070 Ti, Ryzen 9950X, TensorRT FP16, six UE5 scenes): **2.70 ms complete pipeline at 720p** (incl. target G-buffer + shadow-depth generation), vs ExtraSS 3.67 ms, ExtraNet 6.10 ms, Mob-FGSR 0.11 ms (Mob-FGSR faster but much lower quality: PSNR 24.67 vs their 30.43). Quality: **PSNR 30.43 / SSIM 0.9365 / LPIPS 0.0614** avg — beats ExtraSS (28.84/0.9254/0.0714) and ExtraNet (29.82/0.9274/0.0749). **Why it matters to OSS:** (1) a fresh, *code-public*, SIGGRAPH-Asia-level frame-extrapolation baseline in exactly the OSS-FX comparison set (ExtraSS/ExtraNet/Mob-FGSR were already tracked; TFE is the new leader among CNN-extrapolation methods); (2) its **spend-compute-only-where-reprojection-fails** tile gate is the pixel-space cousin of the OSS spawner allocation question and GSReuse's tile-validity gate — third independent instance of the density-allocation-gate pattern (VS-Splat voxel selection is the fourth); (3) requires target-time G-buffers (interpolation-flavored despite "extrapolation" name) — the latency caveat OSS-FX's α<1 single-endpoint design avoids; (4) moving-shadow handling is a failure mode OSS's canvas doesn't explicitly model yet. Supplement PDF **mirrored** (`2026-tfe-siggraph26-supplement.pdf`, 8.7 MB, sha256 7df716bc…). Deep-read queued.
