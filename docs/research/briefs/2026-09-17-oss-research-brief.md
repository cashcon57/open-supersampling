### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal

- **[FastFlowGS]** Mallick, Cherdchusakulchai, Ruiz Olle, Mosella-Montoro, Ribeiro-Gomes, Carrasco, and De la Torre (2026). *Racing in Volume with Flow Ensembles* (FastFlowGS + Monaco4D). arXiv:2609.16310 (cs.CV, Sep-14 batch). **Past the bar — appended to BIBLIOGRAPHY.md. ⚠ Coverage gap: published Sep 14 and missed by every prior brief including the Mon-14 listing scan — the series' first confirmed miss; flag for a one-off sweep-coverage audit.**
  > Streaming 4D Gaussian Splatting of **fast-moving subjects from a sparse ring of fixed external cameras** (F1 cars, outdoor, photorealistic UE5 `Monaco4D` benchmark), fusing sparse matches, semi-dense tracks, and dense optical flow — each lifted to 3D with **geometric uncertainty** and combined in a **Kalman-style temporal update**; exceeds the strongest baseline by 12.6% VMAF at 35% greater efficiency (CMU-Panoptic) and improves dynamic-region PSNR up to 18.6% with **28.3% lower per-frame optimization time** (Monaco4D). **Why it matters to OSS:** the uncertainty-weighted multi-signal motion fusion is the exact shape of the v7 spawner's evidence problem — engine MVs, rendered-image motion, and depth each carry different reliability, and FastFlowGS shows per-signal uncertainty + recursive temporal fusion beats any single signal. Joins GSReuse's reliability gate, TFE's tile gate, DecoGS's gradient gate, and VS-Splat in the spend-compute-only-where-things-move family — as its **multi-signal fusion** member. UE5 benchmark provenance also makes it a rendering-loop-adjacent evaluation reference.

### Inference Optimization (backfill)

- **[M3-Ultra-Opt]** Ochiai (2026). *Systematic Optimization of Real-Time Diffusion Model Inference on Apple M3 Ultra*. arXiv:2605.16259 (cs.LG/AI/DC, Feb-10 — backfill, never logged by the series). **Past the bar — appended to BIBLIOGRAPHY.md.**
  > 10-phase systematic optimization of real-time img2img diffusion on M3 Ultra (60-core GPU, 512 GB unified): CoreML conversion, quantization, Token Merging, Neural Engine, compact models (SDXS-512 distilled), interpolation, flow-based frame skipping, distillation → 22.7 FPS at 512². Key findings: **quantization gives no speedup on Apple Silicon, parallel inference is ineffective, the Neural Engine is unsuitable for large models** — the optimization landscape is fundamentally different from CUDA. **Why it matters to OSS:** direct planning input for **#8 cross-vendor kernel ports (Metal lane)** — the ≤1M student on Apple Silicon should bet on CoreML + reparameterized compact convs + pipelining, with quantization demoted from presumed win to measured experiment. Complements MLX-DLSS as the Apple-lane evidence base.
