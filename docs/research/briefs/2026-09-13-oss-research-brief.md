### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal

- **P-GSVC: Layered Progressive 2D Gaussian Splatting for Scalable Image and Video** (Longan Wang, **Yuang Shi**, Wei Tsang Ooi — NUS, same group as GS-STVSR; arXiv:2603.10551, 2026-03-11). First layered progressive 2DGS framework: base layer + successive enhancement layers with coarse-to-fine reconstruction, and a joint training strategy that co-updates Gaussians across layers so inter-layer compatibility and stable progressive reconstruction hold. Unified for both images and video. Why it matters to OSS: **newly surfaced as material** (Mar 2026, found via listing scan). It is the layered-decomposition answer to #12's packed-canvas problem from the group whose covariance-resampling OSS already uses: a layered canvas (base + enhancement) is a direct donor for progressive capture-upload, bandwidth-adaptive streaming, and canvas LoD — same slot D2GV-AR's any-ratio coding targets, but splat-layer-based rather than pruning-based. The joint cross-layer optimization is the mechanism to read: it answers how enhancement Gaussians stay compatible when base Gaussians move (the exact temporal-identity question a layered v7 canvas must answer).
- **CVT-GS: Learning to Simplify 3D Gaussian Splatting with Centroidal Voronoi Tessellation** (Li, Li, Peng, Wang, Zhu, Li, Liu, Wang, Pang; arXiv:2609.08730, 2026-09-08). Optimization-free **post-hoc** simplification: compresses already-trained 3DGS scenes without retraining, fine-tuning, or architectural change; CVT-based primitive consolidation preserving fidelity. Why it matters to OSS: #12's compactness-mechanism decision is now a **three-way choice** — LinearMask-GS (train-time learned-mask pruning), Compact-FFGS (saliency-guided merging), and now CVT-GS (post-hoc, works on off-the-shelf checkpoints). Post-hoc is operationally attractive for OSS: it could compact trained canvas checkpoints (including old runs) without touching the training loop. Gated as always on temporal-identity, disocclusion quality, and L2-residency validation.
- **Laplacian Frequency Hierarchies for Efficient 3D Gaussian Splatting Training** (Yang, Zhang, Yan, Shi, Wang; arXiv:2609.03334, 2026-09-03). Attacks primitive-count growth during training: fit low-frequency structure, **archive** that Gaussian field, then fit higher-frequency residuals in new fields, compositing components at inference via Laplacian-style reconstruction. Why it matters to OSS: (1) a TruncGradGS-class training-growth control for v7 canvas training — candidate cheap ablation alongside the HyperGS rank regularizer; (2) the archive-and-compose inference pattern is an alternative canvas-residency architecture (hot high-freq field + archived low-freq layers) that trades a second composite pass for a smaller active primitive set — relevant to #12 if tile-level archival is acceptable.
- Re-checked and rejected this window: GSComplete (2609.08449 — diffusion-prior splat completion; offline class), Tri-DehazeGS (2609.11223), FujinSplat (2609.06017 — smoke domain), AVSplat (2609.05925 — sparse-view feed-forward, below bar vs SR3R/HyperGS lineage), TV-SGS/STARS-GS (sparse-view/aerial, known), GradRig (2609.05127 — rig-skinning deformation, engine-asset track at most), Gaussian Light Transport (2609.11430 — LT representation research), Hologram quadratic-phase GS (2609.11434), Heat Kernel Textures (2609.07557), mmWave radar splatting (2609.11894), LangStreet/CVT-adjacent street-scene work (2609.11680-class).
