## New Papers (arXiv)
### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal
- **Hand-4DGS: Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting for 4D Hand Reconstruction from Egocentric Videos** (arXiv:2606.19156v1; 2026-06-17; Jeongmin Bae, Seoha Kim, Marc Pollefeys, Mahdi Rad et al.) — Feed-forward 4D Gaussian hand reconstruction from egocentric video with mesh-guided structural priors and temporal convolutions. Why it matters to OSS: feed-forward dynamic Gaussian reconstruction at ~60 FPS supports OSS's bias toward direct/persistent primitives over per-frame optical-flow stacks; temporal conv + structural priors are worth scanning for lightweight motion encoders.
- **GSPan: A Continuous Gaussian Primitive Representation for Arbitrary-Scale Pansharpening** (arXiv:2606.17722v1; 2026-06-16; Fangyi Li, Xiaoyuan Yang, Yixiao Li, Zongyang Sui et al.) — Represents pansharpening residual details as continuous learnable 2D Gaussian primitives rendered back into the HR image. Why it matters to OSS: another 2D-Gaussian-as-continuous-residual-field paper; useful evidence that Gaussian residual/detail fields are becoming a common arbitrary-scale SR primitive, even outside games.
- **PersistGS: Differentiable Physics for Object Permanence in 4D Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2606.03479v1; 2026-06-02; Adrian Ramlal, John S. Zelek) — Couples differentiable rigid-body simulation with dynamic 3DGS to preserve occluded objects when photometric supervision vanishes. Why it matters to OSS: object permanence / occlusion is directly relevant to persistent canvas stability, disocclusion failure cases, and future OSS-FX temporal priors.
- **Already known but re-surfaced:** **GS-STVSR: Ultra-Efficient Continuous Spatio-Temporal Video Super-Resolution via 2D Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2604.18047v1) remains the closest academic reference; keep it as the covariance-resampling baseline rather than a new bibliography add.

### Super-Resolution / Upscaling
- **EvTexture++: Event-Driven Texture Enhancement for Video Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.13580v1; 2026-06-11; Dachun Kai, Jiayao Lu, Yueyi Zhang, Xiaoyan Sun) — Uses event signals specifically for high-frequency texture enhancement in VSR, with an iterative texture enhancement branch. Why it matters to OSS: its event-texture branch is not directly usable without event sensors, but the separation of motion alignment vs high-frequency texture enhancement is a useful teacher/student loss-design analogy.
- **Neural Super-Resolution for Real-time Rendering with Radiance Demodulation** (arXiv:2308.06699v2; 2023-08-13; Jia Li, Ziling Chen, Xiaolong Wu, Lu Wang et al.) — Separates radiance into smoother lighting and high-detail material components, super-resolves lighting, then remodulates for temporally stable real-time rendering SR. Why it matters to OSS: not new, but the arXiv search re-found a real-time rendering SR technique that explicitly demodulates lighting/material; this is an actionable architectural comparison for OSS G-buffer/material-feature inputs.
- **GSPan** above also belongs here: arbitrary-scale SR via rendered Gaussian residuals is a clean comparison point for OSS canvas residual/detail injection.

### Frame Extrapolation / Generation
- **VeloGauss: Learning Physically Consistent Gaussian Velocity Fields from Videos** (arXiv:2605.10567v1; 2026-05-11; Nengbo Lu, Bin Zhao) — Learns physically consistent Gaussian velocity fields from dynamic multi-view videos without explicit physical priors. Why it matters to OSS: per-Gaussian velocity fields overlap OSS v7 native time/cross-covariance and could inform velocity/acceleration regularizers for α<1 extrapolation.
- **CausalGS: Learning Physical Causality of 3D Dynamic Scenes with Gaussian Representations** (arXiv:2605.10586v1; 2026-05-11; Nengbo Lu, Minghua Pan) — Learns causal dynamics of dynamic 3D scenes with Gaussian representations and inverse-physics inference. Why it matters to OSS: future-frame causality/predictive Gaussian dynamics are aligned with OSS-FX, but the paper is heavier/scene-reconstruction oriented rather than exact-engine-motion-vector game inference.
- **ANVIL: Accelerator-Native Video Interpolation via Codec Motion Vector Priors** (arXiv:2603.26835v3; 2026-03-27; Shibo Liu) — Uses codec motion-vector priors to prealign frames, removing learned optical flow/spatial sampling from mobile NPU video interpolation. Why it matters to OSS: strong deployment analogue for reusing supplied motion vectors and deleting unsupported/memory-bound operators from the accelerator graph; maps directly to OSS engine-motion-vector strategy.
