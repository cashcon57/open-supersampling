## New Papers (arXiv)

### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal
- **Learning Efficient 4D Gaussian Representations from Monocular Videos with Flow Splatting** (arXiv:2606.29976, Shengjun Zhang, Jinzhao Li, Xin Fei, Yueqi Duan, 2026-06-29; previously tracked) — Extends time-varying means/covariances with a velocity field and flow-splatting supervision for faster dynamic 4D-GS. **Why it matters to OSS:** direct comparison target for v7's native time-axis / `V_xt` cross-covariance motion encoding.
- **Resonant Brane Splatting for Arbitrary-Scale Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.29453v2, Giulio Federico et al., 2026-06-28; previously tracked; v2 surfaced today) — Replaces plain low-pass 2D Gaussians with higher-order Gaussian-Hermite “Brane” primitives to encode high-frequency detail with fewer overlapping splats. **Why it matters to OSS:** candidate v7 primitive/raster ablation for edge/detail recovery if licensing/code clarity improves.
- **Learning to Adaptively Allocate Gaussians for Arbitrary-Scale Image Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.29400, Giulio Federico et al., 2026-06-28; previously tracked) — QuADA-GS predicts adaptive Gaussian allocation/densification from LR features using neural routing and hierarchical pointer convolution. **Why it matters to OSS:** direct feed-forward density-budget comparison for parent-child/loss-adaptive v7 canvas allocation.
- **Envision4D: Envisioning Visual Futures via Feed-forward 4D Gaussian Splatting for Autonomous Driving** (arXiv:2606.10656, Qi Song et al., 2026-06-09; already tracked) — Feed-forward future-scene extrapolation with future-pose prediction, in-layer temporal attention, and conditioned motion lifting. **Why it matters to OSS:** relevant long-horizon extrapolation baseline for OSS-FX beyond alpha-interpolation, despite autonomous-driving domain mismatch.

### Super-Resolution / Upscaling
- **AVSR-Diff: Scale-Agnostic Diffusion Priors for Temporally Consistent Arbitrary-Scale Video Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2607.00987, Geunhyuk Youk, Jeonghyeok Do, Dayeon Kim, Jihyong Oh, Munchurl Kim, 2026-07-01; already appended) — Separates scale-agnostic latent denoising from continuous coordinate rendering, with temporally gated feature recurrence and scale-aware Fourier refinement for arbitrary-scale VSR. **Why it matters to OSS:** strong teacher/pseudo-target candidate for temporal arbitrary-scale SR; not a shipping runtime path, but its continuous decoder + temporal gating are directly relevant to v7 training and student distillation.
- **EvTexture++: Event-Driven Texture Enhancement for Video Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.13580, Dachun Kai et al., 2026-06-11; already tracked) — Uses event-camera high-frequency spatiotemporal details for texture enhancement while shifting event signals away from motion refinement. **Why it matters to OSS:** useful analogy for separating motion alignment from texture/detail losses, though event sensors make it non-runtime-relevant.
- **Neural Super-Resolution for Real-time Rendering with Radiance Demodulation** (arXiv:2308.06699, Jia Li et al., 2023; previously tracked) — Separates lighting/radiance from material detail and uses motion/occlusion-aware recurrent SR for real-time rendering. **Why it matters to OSS:** still a key game-rendering baseline for G-buffer/material decomposition and temporal stability.
- **Efficient INT8 Single-Image Super-Resolution via Deployment-Aware Quantization and Teacher-Guided Training** (arXiv:2604.20291, Pham Phuong Nam Nguyen et al., 2026-04-22; already tracked) — Low-resolution-space reparameterizable student with Charbonnier/DCT/confidence-weighted distillation and QAT on the fused deploy graph. **Why it matters to OSS:** closest recent INT8 student-training recipe for ≤1M-param OSS-FX distillation, even though it is SISR not temporal canvas SR.

### Frame Extrapolation / Generation
- **CausalGS: Learning Physical Causality of 3D Dynamic Scenes with Gaussian Representations** (arXiv:2605.10586, Nengbo Lu, Minghua Pan, 2026; previously tracked) — Uses inferred velocity/material properties with differentiable physics for long-term future frame extrapolation in dynamic 3DGS. **Why it matters to OSS:** potential long-horizon regularization idea beyond single alpha/time-query interpolation.
- **VeloGauss: Learning Physically Consistent Gaussian Velocity Fields from Videos** (arXiv:2605.10567, Nengbo Lu, Bin Zhao, 2026; previously tracked) — Learns per-Gaussian velocity fields plus global physical constraints for future-frame extrapolation. **Why it matters to OSS:** overlaps v7's idea of motion as a primitive property; candidate velocity-loss reference.
- **ReFrame: Layer Caching for Accelerated Inference in Real-Time Rendering** (arXiv:2506.13814, Lufei Liu, Tor M. Aamodt, 2025; already tracked) — Reuses intermediate features across temporally coherent neural rendering workloads, reporting speedups for encoder-decoder style networks. **Why it matters to OSS:** candidate cache/reuse baseline for the teacher/student parts around a persistent canvas, especially if the neural head remains above budget.
- **RSTT: Real-time Spatial Temporal Transformer for Space-Time Video Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2203.14186, Zhicheng Geng, Luming Liang, Tianyu Ding, Ilya Zharkov, 2022; already appended) — Older single-model STVSR transformer combining spatial upscaling and temporal interpolation with a reusable encoder dictionary. **Why it matters to OSS:** useful historical baseline for unified SR+frame-generation positioning, though 4.5M params / 26.2fps is far above OSS's target budget.

### Inference Optimization
- **Unfolding Once is Enough: A Deployment-Friendly Transformer Unit for Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2308.02794, Yong Liu et al., 2023; previously tracked) — DITN/UFONE removes redundant transformer operators and includes TensorRT-oriented layernorm substitution/fusion. **Why it matters to OSS:** reference for simplifying HAT-like teacher blocks or student distillation targets around actual deployment kernels.
- **Efficient INT8 Single-Image Super-Resolution via Deployment-Aware Quantization and Teacher-Guided Training** (arXiv:2604.20291, Pham Phuong Nam Nguyen et al., 2026-04-22; already tracked) — Quantization-aware training on the fused deploy graph plus teacher-guided DCT/confidence losses. **Why it matters to OSS:** concrete QAT curriculum to adapt when the v7 teacher distills into FP8/INT8 TensorRT.
- **ITNet: A Learnable Integral Transform That Subsumes Convolution, Attention, and Recurrence** (arXiv:2606.19538, Ashim Dhor, Rasel Mondal, Pin-Yu Chen, 2026; previously tracked) — Unified operator with tiled kernel fusion, Monte Carlo integration, and low-rank factorization. **Why it matters to OSS:** speculative but relevant to future fused canvas-update kernels that blend convolution, attention, and recurrence.
- **NAWQ-SR / INT8 SR challenge / integer-arithmetic SR papers** (older, surfaced by query) — quantized SR deployment references. **Why it matters to OSS:** reinforce that INT8/FP8 student quality requires quantization-aware loss/calibration and layer-wise precision policy, not just export flags.
