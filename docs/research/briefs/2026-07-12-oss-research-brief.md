## New Papers (arXiv)

### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal
- **On the Design of Mixture-of-Experts for Dynamic Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2607.08250v1, In-Hwan Jin, Hyeongju Mun, Joonsoo Kim, Kugjin Yun, 2026-07-09) — Already tracked from 2026-07-11; proposes multi-deformation experts for heterogeneous dynamic-scene motion. Why it matters to OSS: strongest current lead for a motion-router ablation if v7's single native-time `V_xt` encoding underfits mixed static/rigid/articulated/transient objects.
- **Track2Map: Online Deformable SLAM with Motion-Aware Pose Optimization in Robotic Surgery** (arXiv:2607.08408v1, Tianyi Song et al., 2026-07-09) — Online deformable 3DGS with joint camera trajectory and deformable scene optimization under noisy priors. Why it matters to OSS: off-domain but useful as a robustness reference for canvas updates when engine motion vectors are wrong/missing; not a direct game-SR baseline.
- **NoDrift3R: Raymap-Guided Coupling for Drift-Robust Unposed Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction** (arXiv:2607.07168v1, Xiangyu Sun et al., 2026-07-08) — Tackles accumulated drift in long-sequence feed-forward 3DGS reconstruction. Why it matters to OSS: a warning about persistent-state drift; OSS's exact engine MVs should be a differentiator, but long-running canvas drift still needs metrics.
- **MVFusion-GS: Motion-Variance Guided Temporal Attention for High-Quality Dynamic Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2607.01578v1, Jianwei Hu et al., 2026-07-02) — Already tracked; per-Gaussian motion variance and temporal attention separate difficult dynamic regions. Why it matters to OSS: direct comparison for `V_xt`, density, and pruning heuristics.
- **World from Motion: Generative Dynamic Gaussian Reconstruction from Monocular Video** (arXiv:2607.01202v1, Liyuan Zhu et al., 2026-07-01) — Generative dynamic Gaussian reconstruction from monocular video with dense geometry/motion conditioning. Why it matters to OSS: off-runtime, but a teacher/offline prior for dynamic-object canvas behavior.

### Super-Resolution / Upscaling
- **Learning to Adaptively Allocate Gaussians for Arbitrary-Scale Image Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.29400v1, Giulio Federico, Giuseppe Amato, Claudio Gennaro, Fabio Carrara, Marco Di Benedetto, 2026-06-28) — Already in bibliography; feed-forward adaptive allocation of 2D Gaussians for ASR. Why it matters to OSS: direct ablation target for v7 parent-child loss-adaptive density and packed-canvas budgeting.
- **Resonant Brane Splatting for Arbitrary-Scale Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2606.29453v2, Giulio Federico, Giuseppe Amato, Claudio Gennaro, Fabio Carrara, 2026-06-28 / revised 2026-07-01) — Already tracked; higher-order Brane/Gaussian-Hermite primitives improve high-frequency representation. Why it matters to OSS: primitive-level candidate to reduce splat count or improve edge/detail recovery, if raster cost stays bounded.
- **AVSR-Diff: Scale-Agnostic Diffusion Priors for Temporally Consistent Arbitrary-Scale Video Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2607.00987v1, Geunhyuk Youk et al., 2026-07-01) — Already tracked; diffusion-prior arbitrary-scale VSR with temporal consistency. Why it matters to OSS: possible offline teacher/pseudo-target source, not a shipping inference path.
- **PhyMRI-SR: Toward Physics-Aware MRI Image Super-Resolution** (arXiv:2607.06238v1, Lihua Wei et al., 2026-07-07) — Already tracked; MRI SR using physics-aware assumptions and 2D-Gaussian/coordinate reconstruction. Why it matters to OSS: weak/off-domain; only useful as degradation-physics-aware SR inspiration.

### Frame Extrapolation / Generation
- **MASS: Motion-Aligned Selective Scan for Refinement in Flow-Based Video Frame Interpolation** (arXiv:2606.27718v1, Jun-Sang Yoo, Seung-Won Jung, 2026-06-26) — Flow-based VFI refinement using motion-aligned selective scan to handle large nonlinear motion and occlusion ambiguity. Why it matters to OSS: material baseline for OSS-FX artifact handling; compare selective state-space refinement against Gaussian-canvas alpha-time rendering around disocclusions and nonlinear motion.
- **LongE2V: Long-Horizon Event-based Video Reconstruction, Prediction, and Frame Interpolation with Video Diffusion Models** (arXiv:2607.08770v1, Cheng-De Fan, Chun-Wei Tuan Mu, Chen-Wei Chang, Chin-Yang Lin, 2026-07-09) — Uses pretrained video diffusion models for long-horizon event-video reconstruction, prediction, and interpolation. Why it matters to OSS: mostly off-domain/event-camera; useful only as a long-horizon stability/temporal-prior lead, not a game runtime competitor.
- **UniRED: Unified RGB-D Video Frame Interpolation with Event Guidance** (arXiv:2606.24282v1, Yinuo Zhang et al., 2026-06-23) — Already surfaced in prior scans; unified RGB-D/event-guided VFI. Why it matters to OSS: conditioning/artifact reference for depth+motion guided frame generation, but game inputs differ.
- **DLGStream: Dynamic Language-embedded Guassian Splatting for Open-vocabulary Enabled Free-viewpoint Video Streaming** (arXiv:2606.28840v1, Zhihui Ke et al., 2026-06-27) — Already tracked; dynamic Gaussian streaming with temporal redundancy reduction and 4D frame interpolation. Why it matters to OSS: canvas serialization / active-mask streaming reference.

### Inference Optimization
- **QuantSR+: Pushing the Limit of Quantized Image Super-Resolution Networks** (arXiv:2605.22351v1, Haotong Qin et al., 2026-05-21) — Already in bibliography; low-bit quantization for SR networks. Why it matters to OSS: direct reference for ultra-low-bit student deployment and calibration.
- **Efficient INT8 Single-Image Super-Resolution via Deployment-Aware Quantization and Teacher-Guided Training** (arXiv:2604.20291v1, Pham Phuong Nam Nguyen et al., 2026-04-22) — Already tracked in earlier bibliography modifications; deployment-aware quantized SR student. Why it matters to OSS: directly relevant to ≤1M-param INT8/FP8 student distillation.
- **teasr: training-efficient any-step diffusion transformer for real-world image super-resolution** (arXiv:2606.16188v1, Xiang Gao et al., 2026-06-15) — Already tracked; one/any-step diffusion SR training-efficiency method. Why it matters to OSS: teacher/pseudo-target recipe only; runtime too heavy/stochastic.
- No new TensorRT/kernel-fusion SR paper surfaced in this run beyond already-tracked quantization/deployment leads. Keep focus on deployment-aware quantization, Decoder-Free Distillation, QuantSR+, and real fused kernels rather than nominal INT8 export.
