### Gaussian Splatting / Temporal
- **KoalaGS — Complexity-Aware Continuous Level of Detail for Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2610.28615, Beche, R.; de Charette, R., 2026-10-20) — one trained primitive set stays effective when only a prefix of Gaussians is rendered; allocates capacity by input-image complexity (simple regions hold too many Gaussians and degrade as badly as complex ones at low budgets). *Why it matters to OSS:* direct analogue of the H004 "budgeted tile scheduler" and the Pico/Standard/Heavy tier story — a Gaussian-ordering/priority rule could let one canvas serve several compute budgets, and image-complexity-driven allocation is a cheap prior for the spawner. 3DGS scene-level, not temporal; idea transfer only.
- **RLSplat — Resource-Linear Budget Control for Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting** (arXiv:2610.27712, Kim, I.; Joo, M.; Park, J.; Kim, S. et al., 2026-10-21) — budget-controllable feed-forward 3DGS where *inference cost* also scales linearly with the Gaussian budget (prior methods predict a dense set then prune, so latency is flat). *Why it matters:* OSS's spawner is the same shape of problem — predicting then pruning Gaussians defeats a <2 ms budget; worth reading for how they predict directly at budget.
- **HiQGS — Hierarchical Spatiotemporal Querying for Feed-Forward Gaussian Reconstruction** (arXiv:2610.27604, Miao, Q.; Cheng, Q.; Guo, Y.; Xiao, Y. et al., 2026-10-21) — queries structured by temporal relation to the requested timestamp, multi-scale. *Why it matters:* closest to the v7 time-axis slice (α<1) conceptually (timestamp-conditioned Gaussian prediction); offline/3D, low priority; skim only alongside dGS/dBS (2610.22778).
- Low relevance, not added: SplatFlux (2610.28257, latent→4DGS, generative), Occlusion-aware densification (2610.27108), GTSM thermal splatting (2610.26918), Codec-compatible 3DGS compression (2610.26920), assorted 3DGS avatar/scene papers.

## GitHub / Open Source
- **`zgauthier2000/fsr4-shader-performance-overrides`** (274★, created 2026-10-01, pushed 10-21) — replaces FSR 4.1.1's slowest shaders with faster community shaders; separate DLLs for RX 7000/6000 and for iGPUs (incl.
